# MEMO-01 — Annual review — pump casings

**Date:** 2024-11-12 · **Author:** Buyer, castings · **Supplier (as written):** Corranby Foundry Works · **Company:** Brightkeel Pumps Manufacturing W.L.L.

Corranby supplies about 60 % of our grey-iron pump casings from its plant near Sitra. On-time delivery this year was 94 %, with two late batches in August caused by a furnace repair.

The contract includes a product indemnity capped at BHD 250,000. Corranby shares the casing work with Talbeck, which holds the remaining volume and is fully qualified for the same patterns.

No change to the allocation is proposed.
