# K-11 Industrial-gap panel: Group 5, C5-A Supplier Risk Control Tower (DRAFT)

**Status: DRAFT, prepared by Hussain's Claude on 2026-11-26. Not yet confirmed by Hussain.** Built from canvas field 11 and what the tool does now. No real company, product or statistic is named.

| Line | Our entry |
|---|---|
| 1. What our classroom version **assumes** | The three sources are complete, correct and fixed as of 2026-10-31: the supplier master, the assessment log and 24 memos. Buyers' memos describe the contracts accurately, so we read indemnity from a memo sentence, not from the contract itself. Supplier names differ only in ways RR-C5 §2 can clean up. Claude's extraction is checked by a quotation match and a person's re-read, which is enough for 24 short memos. One group runs the tool by hand, on one laptop, with no other users. |
| 2. What a **real system additionally needs** | **Data:** the real signed contracts from a contract system (with a lawyer confirming what each indemnity clause means), and assessment results and approvals live from the ERP or supplier portal, not a fixed CSV. **Integration:** a shared supplier ID across all systems, so names never have to be matched by text, and a named owner who keeps supplier records clean. **Scale:** thousands of documents in many formats and languages, updated every day, so the extraction needs ongoing sampling and measured error rates, not a one-off re-read. **Governance:** access control (contract terms are confidential), an audit trail of who changed what and when, version control for the rules, and a review whenever the rules or the AI model change. |
| 3. **Where the human stays**, and why | The tool only shows risk; the category director and buyers decide what to do about it: find a second source, renegotiate an indemnity cap, schedule an assessment or accept the risk. These decisions involve cost, contracts and supplier relationships the data does not cover. A person also resolves every case the tool refuses: a supplier name that does not match (§2), a part family it cannot place (for example MEMO-20's "bronze range"), or two memos on the same date that disagree. The tool never changes a supplier's approval status. |
| 4. (optional) Deployed / Emerging / Hype label | Not filled: it needs a cited, dated source, and we have not chosen one. |
