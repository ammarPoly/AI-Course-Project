# K-06 — Test record (spare copy)

Your pack has its own copy with its acceptance questions already entered — use that one. This spare copy is for any extra record. **How to fill it:** K-05 §2.

**Rules**
- Write the **expected result and its rule or clause before the run**, and who wrote it.
- **The chat that produced an answer is not its check.** Independent routes: recompute in Excel; a fresh session by another member working from the sources; a person reading the source.
- After every change, re-run the cases that should **not** change and write "no change was observed on the regression cases" — never "nothing else changed".
- MISMATCH and OBSERVED FAILURE are honest results. Nothing is marked as passed afterwards for a step that was not run.

Types: ACCEPTANCE · TRAP · REFUSAL · EDGE · EXTENSION · REGRESSION · INDEPENDENT CHECK. Verdicts: MATCH · MISMATCH · OBSERVED FAILURE · NOT RUN.

**By the end of Week 9 the course expects (not a marking scheme):** every acceptance question answered · the problems you found in the pack, with evidence · at least one refusal · all extension cases · at least one regression run per change after Week 8 · at least one independent check per output type.
