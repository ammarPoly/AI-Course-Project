# K-12 — Showcase preparation sheet and guide (Weeks 9–10)

## 1. Mandatory content
- run it live where feasible
- what goes in
- what the AI does
- what comes out
- one failure or limit, demonstrated or discussed
- what still requires a human
- what it would take to become a real industrial system

## 2. How to prepare (from the start of Week 9)
1. **Freeze first** (CP-9a): you rehearse and show the frozen version, not work in progress.
2. **Write the run of show** below, minute by minute, with who speaks and which file or screen is open.
3. **Prepare one answer to each of the six questions** — one sentence each, with the evidence that backs it (test ID or file). For "What type of system?", be ready to explain one design decision from your decision log (K-02) and one fix you made after a failed test.
4. **Rehearse the trap round:** run three cases your audience might throw at you — including one your tool should refuse. A refusal on screen is a good answer.
5. **Make demo insurance:** screenshots or a short screen recording of the frozen version doing the core run, saved in `06_SHOWCASE/`.
6. **Time it** at the dress rehearsal (CP-9b) against the slot the instructor sets.

## 5. Demo insurance and the switch rule
The frozen version plus screenshots or a recording. **Switch rule:** if the live run stalls for about a minute, switch to the insurance and say so — that is not a failure; hiding it would be.
