# K-14 — Troubleshooting and help

## 1. The help procedure
1. **Your pack's `OPTIONAL_HELP/`** — when you are stuck on the problem itself. Start at Level 1 (questions); Level 3 holds concrete tips. **This card** — most technical problems below have a labelled fallback.
2. **Your group** — the data lead for pack questions, the build lead for tool questions, the evidence lead for "where is that file?".
3. **The consult queue** — bring a consult slip (K-09): your question, what you tried, your evidence. Blocked groups are seen first. You leave with **one written next step**.
4. **Between sessions** — the course channel the instructor announces. Never share a password or let someone else sign in to your account.

A consult points you to the rule card, the canvas or a test. It does not solve the project, and the instructor will not tell you whether something in your pack is a planted problem.

## 2. Common problems and fallbacks
| Problem | What to do |
|---------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Cannot unzip the pack | Re-download; ask the data lead for the local copy. |
| A CSV looks garbled in Excel, or numbers lose their decimal point | Reopen it through Excel's import route (Data → From Text/CSV) and choose "." as the decimal separator; the pack CSVs use plain headers. |
| An amount is treated as text — it sits at the left of the cell, a `SUM` leaves it out, `=ISNUMBER(cell)` shows FALSE, or a unit such as "BHD" is typed beside the number | Do not retype, delete or silently convert it. If your rule card says how such amounts count (for example a total "as the file opens" and a total with every amount read as a number), follow it exactly; otherwise record it as a finding with its row, and bring it to the consult queue. |
| Project upload refused or too large | Attach the batch or section files per chat (your pack's `OPTIONAL_HELP/`, Level 3, has the fallback). |
| Image upload limit | One document per message in the same chat. |
| Skill does not load | Check the capability setting and the Skill's description; then run the method as a pasted prompt, labelled "method used as prompt, not a Skill". |
| Artifact will not save or run | Do that step another way, for example a sheet in Excel with visible formulas. |
| Usage limit | Pair with a member who has headroom, wait for the reset the screen shows, or do the Excel / manual step. **Buy nothing.** |
| Any API-key or billing message | **Stop and tell the tutor.** |
| Cannot sign in to your own account | Pair with a member for this session; tell the tutor. Never use someone else's account. |
| Claude Code or Cowork trouble | They are allowed, never required. Do that step another way (Claude chat and Excel), record the switch in your decision log, and bring the problem to the consult queue. |
| Your prototype needs software that is not on your laptop | Choose a route that needs nothing new: a web page Claude writes that opens in your browser, Excel, or Claude chat. Buy nothing and use no API key; ask at the consult queue before installing anything. |
| Your prototype and your hand check disagree | That is a test result, not a disaster: record MISMATCH (K-05), look at the intermediate values to find the first step that differs, fix that step in a new version, and re-run your regression cases. |
| Laptop failure | Pair; work from the group folder. |
| Lost work | `03_VERSIONS/` — this is why you save a version before every risky change. |
| Something in the pack looks wrong | Do not edit the pack. Note it (file, line), carry on with the rest, and tell the instructor at the consult queue. Any correction comes as a new pack version to your group. |
| A member is absent | Their cover runs their step; catch them up from `00_ADMIN/` and the stand-up card. |
| The group disagrees, or work is unequal | Go back to the roles sheet (K-03); tell the instructor early — not at the showcase. |
